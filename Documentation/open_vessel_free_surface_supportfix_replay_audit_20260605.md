Date: 2026-06-05

Scope: targeted follow-up to `Documentation/open_vessel_free_surface_test02_test10_root_cause_report_20260605.md`. This audit covers the active pressure-support fix, direct source-to-replay pressure-update checks, centered pressure-update statistics, one targeted pressure-stabilization policy comparison, an active/wet pressure-policy branch-shaping comparison, the accepted-step cut-context transition audit, the Newton pressure residual diagnostic, a VMS/PSPG disable control, a pressure-row support audit, a pressure-row constraint-coverage audit, an aggregate active pressure support-rank guard, a proposed Newton pressure-update/support diagnostic with row-action and pressure-subblock null contributors, pressure-update neighborhood diagnostics, a pressure-row contribution diagnostic, operator-level pressure matrix-support attribution including a VMS/PSPG pressure-gradient split, operator-level aggregate pressure-row matrix summaries, exact top-row operator provenance with direct PSPG support ratios, topology flags, patch-neighbor profiles, and combined-policy neighbor comparisons, diagnostic scale/form controls for that PSPG pressure-gradient term, a diagnostic wall-boundary PSPG support probe, a pre-commit pressure-update rejection probe, a diagnostic free-surface pressure-reference probe, synthetic retained-support PSPG hydrostatic, solve-amplification, constant-null boundary pair-completion, shared-support completion, active-support completion, shared-row Schur support completion, solver-level shared-row Schur graph-completion replay, existing-edge balance, shared-row Schur plus existing-edge balance hybrid, and incident-support count-balance patch proxies, and a local pressure-edge completion predictor for Test02/Test10 replay windows.

- `Code/Source/solver/Application/Core/ApplicationDriver.cpp`

Set `SVMP_ACTIVE_PRESSURE_UPDATE_REJECT_ON_TRIGGER=1` with a positive threshold to reject a converged candidate before `TimeLoop` commits it.

## Prototype Evaluation

No new default formulation prototype is recommended from this pass. Three tempting narrow prototypes were ruled out as too narrow by the targeted evidence:

- A zero/weak support-row clamp is useful as a causality probe, but top-update correlation still leaves above-threshold positive-coupled rows with large accepted updates.
- Test02 has no zero-velocity-coupled unconstrained pressure rows in the all-row support replay, and its largest full-wet spike has positive velocity coupling, so a Test10-style zero-row support fix alone would miss the largest Test02 event.
- The new pressure self-block correlation shows Test02's largest class is boundary rows with positive velocity coupling but weak pressure self support, while its tiny-cut weak-velocity rows have positive pressure self support. That points at boundary pressure/null behavior, not a generic pressure self-block threshold.
- The pressure self-block clamp probe confirms this warning. It greatly improves the short Test10 replay, but in Test02 it trades the original full-wet MPa row for an even larger tiny-cut spike.
- The combined weak-self plus weak-velocity clamp removes the Test10 spike almost completely, but makes the Test02 one-step replay fail nonlinear convergence with a large pressure residual. This rules out promoting the support-rank clamp family as the formulation fix.
- The pressure-update neighborhood diagnostic shows the two target cases are not identical local shapes. Test02's largest full-wet row is a strongly amplified boundary point relative to its one-ring patch, while Test10's largest row is part of a coherent same-signed boundary patch. The formulation fix must handle both local boundary-row amplification and coherent weak-support boundary modes.
- The row-action contributor diagnostic narrows the mechanism further: both max rows are pressure-block dominated. Test02 is dominated by the target diagonal pressure term, while Test10 is a pressure-pressure stencil mode with small velocity action. This argues against a velocity-only support fix and toward pressure-block/support consistency on active boundary pressure rows.
- The pressure-subblock null diagnostic rules out a constant pressure/gauge row-sum leakage path at the sampled maxima. The pressure rows preserve a near-zero signed pressure row sum, but the nonconstant local pressure action carries essentially all of the pressure self action.
- The constrained/unconstrained row split rules out constrained wall-velocity support as the hidden driver for the sampled Test02/Test10 problematic pressure rows. The remaining support is already the post-constraint solve-matrix support, so the next fix needs to change the formulation/support balance rather than recover eliminated wall-velocity columns.
- The free-surface pressure-reference probe rules out the missing explicit generated-interface pressure trace as the direct source for the known worst rows. The probe assembled globally but contributed `0.0` at Test02 row `10676` and Test10 row `3356`, leaving the target on active cut-volume boundary pressure-block behavior.
- The wall pressure-flux diagnostic adds the natural boundary-flux counterpart of the direct PSPG pressure-gradient term, but it still leaves both full-wet max rows above guard. It lowers the full-gradient Test02 maximum only to `359914.589458 Pa` and Test10 to `571.444348 Pa`, with pressure-self-only sampled support and zero velocity coupling at both failing rows.
- A pressure-gauge/anchor-only change is not credible for the replay spike because active/wet median-removed updates remain close to raw maxima, so the bad update is a localized pressure-shape change rather than a constant gauge offset.
- Disabling or switching the existing pressure ghost-penalty policy is already ruled out by the absolute/incremental/disabled controls and by the contribution diagnostic at the accepted rows.

The next implementation attempt should therefore be a pressure formulation/support consistency change that preserves hydrostatic/linear pressure patch behavior, not a post-assembly update clamp or another scalar penalty/gauge adjustment.
